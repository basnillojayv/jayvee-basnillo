Partner / sponsor / employer logo row on white with generous air. When you have no logo file, pass just `name` — it renders as plain type, which is the sanctioned fallback (never redraw a third-party mark).

```jsx
<LogoWall label="OC FELLOWS IS SPONSORED BY"
  logos={[{ name: 'Edwards Lifesciences' }, { name: 'Bank of America' }]} />
```
