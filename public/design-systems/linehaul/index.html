Brand DNA

LineHaul Station turns commercial truck terminals into a shared-use freight relay platform — a Modern-Day Pony Express. The identity must read masculine, authoritative, rugged — yet modern. Premium interior-commercial polish, never sterile tech minimalism. Cool. Hip. Exclusive. Comfortable.

Company Overview

America's first national, shared-use truck terminal network. We build first-class, full-service Private Terminals in key markets and sell the space in increments — so carriers, brokers, and fleets of every size get a superior terminal at a fraction of the cost of building their own.

A shared-economy model — like a private club for terminal space. Pay a refundable participation fee, pay daily dues, and use the network whenever you need it. The first Hub opens in West Memphis, AR, one block from the I-40 / I-55 interchange.

96%of carriers run fewer than 20 trucks — and will never build a terminal

Logo

The LH shield is the heart of the identity — a chrome-and-steel monogram with a Steel Blue gradient crown over a carbon base. Three lockups cover every application. The white-text lockups run on Carbon Black; the black-text lockups run on light surfaces.

Primary Lockups · On Carbon

Clear Space & Minimum Size
Keep clear space equal to the height of the shield’s crown on all sides. Minimum width: 120px digital / 1in print for the horizontal lockup.
Misuse
✕ Recolor the mark
Never fill the shield with Fuel Orange or flat colors.
✕ Stretch or distort
Scale proportionally only — never squash or skew.
✕ White text on light
Use the black-text lockup on light surfaces; white-text on carbon.
✕ Add effects
No drop shadows, glows, rotation, or outlines.
