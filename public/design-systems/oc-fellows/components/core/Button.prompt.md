Pill-shaped action button — use `primary` (orange) for the single most important action on a view, `secondary` (navy) for the next one down, `outline`/`quiet` for tertiary links-as-buttons, and `inverse` on navy or photographic backgrounds.

```jsx
<Button href="/apply">Apply</Button>
<Button variant="secondary" size="lg">Meet the OC Fellows</Button>
<Button variant="quiet" icon={<i data-lucide="arrow-right" />} iconPosition="right">Learn more</Button>
```

Copy is Title Case, 1–3 words. Hover darkens one step; press nudges down 1px. Never two orange buttons in one section.
