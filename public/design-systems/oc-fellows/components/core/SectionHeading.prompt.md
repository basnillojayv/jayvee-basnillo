Opens a page section: eyebrow claim, plain heading, short intro. Every major band on the site uses one.

```jsx
<SectionHeading
  eyebrow="How OC Fellows Transform Lives"
  title="Impact Stories"
  intro="Stories from OC Fellows showcase growth, purpose, and connection."
/>
```

`align="center"` for full-width bands, left for asymmetric two-column layouts. `tone="dark"` on navy.
