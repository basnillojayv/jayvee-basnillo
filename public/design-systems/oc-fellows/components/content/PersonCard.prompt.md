Fellow or team-member card. `variant="fellow"` is a 3:4 portrait with a class-year pill; `variant="team"` is a circular crop with a role line.

```jsx
<PersonCard name="Parshva Adani" year="2026" photo="…" variant="fellow" />
<PersonCard name="Peggy Wolff" meta="Director, OC Fellows" photo="…" variant="team" />
```

Fellows grid runs five or six across; team runs four across. Photos must be real event or headshot photography.
